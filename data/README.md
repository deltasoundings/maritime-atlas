Maritime Research Atlas

Delta Soundings | Undersea Security OSINT — Project 001

An open-source, interactive atlas examining deep-sea mining activity in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone (CCZ), with plans to expand into undersea cables and maritime research activity.

Status: Phase 1 — Initial Development

Project Overview
The Clarion-Clipperton Zone spans approximately 4.5 million km² of the eastern Pacific Ocean and contains significant polymetallic nodule deposits. This project aims to consolidate publicly available geographic, contractual, and environmental data into an accessible, source-documented map.

Objectives
-Map CCZ boundaries and International Seabed Authority (ISA) exploration contract areas.
-Integrate environmental, bathymetric, and maritime activity data.
-Document dataset sources, limitations, and geographic accuracy.
-Publish a freely accessible interactive atlas.

Tools & Methods
-Python: Data processing and analysis
-GeoPandas & pandas: Geospatial and tabular data management
-Folium: Interactive map development
-Open-source intelligence (OSINT): Public data collection and verification

Development
The project is currently in its initial setup phase. Development will proceed from a basic interactive map to validated geographic layers and a publicly accessible website.
All datasets will be documented with their sources, publication dates, licenses, and known limitations. Official geographic boundaries will be distinguished from approximate or illustrative locations.

Disclaimer
